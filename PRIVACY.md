# Privacy

PhotoSize works entirely on your phone.

- **No network access.** The app does not declare the `INTERNET` permission, so Android
  will not let it connect to anything. There are no ads, analytics, crash reporters or
  accounts.
- **Your photos stay on the device.** Pictures you pick or take are processed in memory.
  A camera capture is written to the app's private cache only so your camera app can hand
  it over, and is replaced by the next capture.
- **What is stored:** files you save go to `Pictures/PhotoSize` on your phone. Images you
  share are copied to the app's cache so the receiving app can read them. Custom presets
  you create are kept in the app's private storage. Nothing else is kept.
- **Sharing** happens only when you tap Share, and only to the app you choose.
- Uninstalling the app removes its private storage; saved pictures in
  `Pictures/PhotoSize` stay until you delete them.
